# Party Intros

Place one recorded female DJ introduction per party mix in this folder.

The intro filename must match its mix number:
- `mix-01.mp3` introduces `party-mixes/mix-01.mp3`
- `mix-02.mp3` introduces `party-mixes/mix-02.mp3`

Each intro is unique to its matching mix and will play immediately before that mix. Keep the spoken intro and music track as separate files.
